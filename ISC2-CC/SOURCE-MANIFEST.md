## Version/currentness warning

The private Thor material found in Drive predates the ISC2 CC exam outline effective 2026-09-01.

Therefore:

- it is useful for foundational teaching,
- its domain structure must not be treated as the current exam structure,
- old weighting must not be reused as current weighting,
- current official objectives take precedence when conflicts exist,
- missing 2026 topics must be added from current official sources.

## Provenance / redistribution rule

The PDFs are private/user-provided study sources. Do not commit the original PDFs, proprietary figures, long passages or answer material into the repository.

Repository teaching content should be independently written in Thai, with original diagrams and explicit source traceability.
